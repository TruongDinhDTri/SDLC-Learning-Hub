## Core Philosophy

Professional software engineering is NOT "just writing code."

It is: **Safely evolving complex systems over time.**

The real goal is not to make a feature work. The real goal is to make it **reliable, maintainable, scalable, observable, and safe to evolve.**

| Junior Mindset | Senior Mindset |
|----------------|----------------|
| How do I code this? | How do I evolve the system safely? |
| Focus on implementation | Focus on architecture and impact |
| Finish the ticket | Preserve long-term maintainability |
| Local feature thinking | System-wide thinking |
| Happy path only | Failure-aware engineering |

## Understand Business Intent

Before writing code, understand:
- What problem is being solved
- Who needs the feature and why it matters to them
- What business outcome is expected
- What success looks like in measurable terms

**Great engineers solve problems — not just tickets.**

## Questions to Ask

```
Who requested this feature?
Who are the actual users?
What pain or problem exists today without this?
What does success look like concretely?
What metrics should improve?
What edge cases matter to the business?
What are the failure scenarios?
```

## Output to Produce

```
Feature: User profile image upload

Goal: Allow users to personalize their profiles.

Business value: Improve engagement and sense of identity in the product.

Constraints:
- Max 5MB file size
- Images only (jpg, png, webp)
- Secure uploads via presigned URLs
- Must work on mobile

Success metric: 40% of users upload a photo within first week of launch.
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Never Skip This Step">
Every hour spent understanding the feature saves 3 hours of rework later. Features built without business context are expensive guesses — and guesses are expensive to fix in production.
</Callout>
